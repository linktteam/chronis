## 3. Final ordering ladder (Item 3; `backbone/hssm/ordering.py`; RUN, tests 18, mutants 29/29)
- **Rungs:** 1 all features with a direction in the feature registry; 2 `movement` + `time_moving`; 3 `movement`. Score of a regime = sum of `w_f * direction_f * z` (`z` = raw-unit regime mean in units of the window's own mean and sd; directions from the registry, a domain prior, never learned).
- **Weights:** `w_f` proportional to `coverage_f * SNR_f / (1 + SNR_f)`, SNR = between-regime over pooled within-regime variance over the decoded regimes; no outcome label enters.
- **Reliability test:** block bootstrap (B = 200, 7-day blocks, labels fixed, weights and scores recomputed, no refit, seed 0). A rung is reliable iff its modal ordering equals the point ordering with frequency >= **0.90** and the smallest adjacent gap >= **g_min = 0.7** (g_min by the declared rule on 60 development fits; the conditional rule would have given 0.5, kept 0.7 as declared). The highest reliable rung names the regimes unless another reliable rung disagrees; otherwise `verified = False`, rung 0, first-fit order, no activity claim.
- **Benchmark (evaluation seeds 8301..8324, first fit, RUN):** verified 17/24 (70.8%); verified orderings equal to the planted order 17/17 (100%, Wilson 81.6-100: criterion L met on the point estimate, **not demonstrated**). Rung per user: **rung 1: 8302, 8303, 8306, 8308, 8312, 8315, 8316, 8319, 8321, 8323, 8324 (11); rung 2: 8305, 8314, 8317 (3); rung 3: 8301, 8311, 8322 (3); rung 0, unverified: 8304, 8307, 8309, 8310, 8313, 8318, 8320 (7)** (`wp8_evidence/item8_rung_per_user.txt`). The ladder says what fitted regimes mean in activity terms; it does not tell whether the fit is right.

## 4. Lineage design (Item 4; `backbone/hssm/lineage.py`; frozen 2026-11-25 before any evaluation fit)
- **States:** `ALIGNED` (same as before, may be claimed), `CONTINUITY_UNVERIFIED` (old names kept, message "cannot confirm this is the same as before"; the user may pick new names, no response = old names), `K_CHANGED` (no continuity claim; split/merge **candidates** only), `NEW_LINEAGE`. The anchor moves only on `ALIGNED` and `NEW_LINEAGE`. Raw outputs are untouched (A4); new fields are additive (`HSSMResult.lineage`; columns `stable_regime_id`, `lineage_id`, `lineage_epoch`, `continuity_state`, `alignment_margin`, `activity_rung`, `scaler_mean`, `scaler_std`, `expert_review_status`).
- **Distance and thresholds (frozen):** Hungarian on squared distance of regime means in raw units divided by the anchor's per-feature scale (no duration term); identifiability gate **h = 4.0** (smallest pairwise regime separation of both fits, in within-regime sd; declared rule on development data); margin rule `best <= 0.5 * second` for the **baseline** class. **Dual-to-dual and cross-engine thresholds are UNKNOWN** (fewer than 20 gate-passing development alignments: 4 and 16) and flagged `calibrated=False`, so those classes are never `ALIGNED` (reason `threshold_not_calibrated`). Two channels (A6): physical and optional contextual (squared Hellinger), default weight on the second 0, disagreement of two confident channels gives `CONTINUITY_UNVERIFIED`; no real contextual descriptor exists (UNKNOWN whether it helps).
- **Per-alignment margin analysis (RUN):** every alignment is stored with `margin_diff = (C2 - C1)/K` and `margin_ratio = C1/C2`. Development (960 fits): permutation errors among alignments of two recoverable fits begin at **`margin_ratio` about 0.48** (all 4 at >= 0.48; none below), close to the production 0.5; all 242 gate-passing baseline alignments were correct (largest ratio 0.33). **Strict-wrong alignments (a fit that does not recover the regimes) occur from ratio 0.013 (development) and 0.05 (evaluation) upward and up to `margin_diff` 3.2: no margin level makes an alignment safe, so the gate, not the margin, carries the safety.** Held-out check (post-hoc, evaluation B-G, 216 alignments): 0 permutation errors; the 72 gate-passing alignments all have ratio below 0.03 (`wp8_evidence/item8_posthoc_margin_analysis.txt`).
- **Margin below which alignments are wrong:** for recoverable fits about **0.48** (a tighter ratio threshold such as 0.35, above the largest gate-passing ratio of 0.33 in development, would cost almost no coverage; left to the expert, A7). For all fits there is no such margin.
- **Refit cadence (A5):** `RefitPolicy(refit_interval_days = 7)`, bounds 1..28 days. Daily, weekly and 14-day schedules gave the same outcomes on the benchmark (criterion 8 met on day-150 agreement 1.000). The upper bound 28 is a declared limit, not tested with drift.
