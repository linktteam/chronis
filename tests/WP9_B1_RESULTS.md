# WP9 B1 (cold-start certification of prior-anchored fits) and B2 (WP7-R Item 4 versus Item 3): results

Design and levels: `tests/PREDECLARED_CRITERIA_WP9.md` Part 5 (sections 14 to 16, committed before any B1 or B2 fit) and Part 5b (dated after the development run, before any evaluation fit); frozen: `tests/DESIGN_FREEZE_WP9_B1.txt`. Labels: **RUN** = executed here, output in `wp9_evidence/`; **READ**; **UNKNOWN**. All data are generated by the WP5 population generator (K = 2, F = 4, L = 2, Dmax 25, a prior built from the generator's own centre: a **favourable** case). Whether real people sit that close to a prior is **UNKNOWN**. No level or threshold was moved after a result was seen.

## What was built (READ, `backbone/hssm/cold_start_cert.py`, opt-in)
`fit_hssm(..., certify_prior_anchored=True)` (dual model with a shared prior only). A prior-anchored fit that EM ended as `REJECTED_UPDATE` or `MAX_ITER_REACHED`, with every other withhold reason absent, is refitted at three rolling origins (50, 65 and 80% of the rows) and scored one step ahead against a single Gaussian; it is **certified** (`CERTIFIED_PRIOR_ANCHORED`) only when the mean gain minus z x a HAC standard error is above zero (z = 3.0) and the regime means at the last origin are within 0.25 single-Gaussian standard deviations of the full fit. Everything else is `NOT_CERTIFIED` with reasons; any exception or non-finite number fails closed. The certification replaces only the reason `fit_state_not_converged`; every other publish rule still applies. Default off. Cost: three extra dual fits.

## Development (RUN, seeds 9631..9642, 288 lines, `wp9_evidence/b1_dev_analysis.txt`)
**No z in {1, 2, 3} met the declared rule** (controls pooled at most 10% and at most half of the `shrink5` certified share): z = 3.0 was taken by the declared fallback and the shortfall reported (controls pooled 12/57 at z = 3; `shrink5` 3/13 eligible certified). The control validity check failed for the scrambled prior (its shared-only score was 0.905 nats/step better than the true prior's) and passed for the shifted prior (1.054 worse). The smaller margin that a valid-controls-only reading would have given (z = 2) was reported and **not** used.

## B2 (RUN, seeds 9681..9692, 36 fits, `wp9_evidence/b2_analysis.txt`)
Question: why did WP7-R Item 4 publish 11 of 12 `shrink5` fits while Item 3 published 8, 9 and 5 of 24 at 30, 60 and 120 steps? Both call `fit_hssm` identically (READ) but fit different data (Item 3: every user perturbed from the population centre; Item 4: the unperturbed centre). The same call on data with a between-user spread factor f:

| spread factor f | publishable (EM rule) | EM states | withheld for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (Item 4 data, verified identical) | 9/12 = 75% | 12 converged | degenerate duration 3 |
| 0.5 | 5/12 = 42% | 8 converged, 4 REJECTED_UPDATE | not converged 4, degenerate duration 3 |
| 1.0 (Item 3 data) | 4/12 = 33% | 4 converged, 8 REJECTED_UPDATE | not converged 8 |

**Declared reading: explained by the data design** (f = 0 at least 9/12, f = 1.0 at most 6/12, non-increasing). The discrepancy between Item 4 and Item 3 is the between-user spread of the generated people, not the engine, the iterations or the seeds. The Item 4 number (11 of 12) is a favourable-case number; the Item 3 numbers describe typical people at the same prior. The stamp for the owner decision B2 is therefore: a cold-start fit's "publishable" share depends on how far the person is from the prior; at 33% (f = 1.0) a cold-start stamp must not be read as "typical".

Why not eligible (reasons recorded, a fit can have several): converged already 17 / 13 / 16 (nothing to certify), another withhold reason (degenerate duration, collapse, ...) 3 / 5 / 2, too few rows 1 / 0 / 0, gain not demonstrated 3 / 1 / 1, parameters unstable 2 / 1 / 0.

### Declared sentences (RUN)
- **"The cold-start gain survives" (WP7-R item 3 sentence): DOES NOT SURVIVE.** Lengths 60 and 120 are judged and positive, but the 30-step length has n = 7 publishable-after fits (< 8), so it is not judged, and the sentence needs every length judged. On the certified fits alone no length reaches n = 8 (0, 4 and 5 certified fits).
- **Net deployment gain (risk-coverage) shown: NO.** It needs `mean - 3 se > 0` at every length; at 30 steps it is -0.096 (gain +0.303 on all 24 users), at 60 and 120 steps it is positive (+0.217, +0.392).
- **E6 certificate precision:** 27/27 = 100% (Wilson 88-100%) of the certified fits of all arms had a positive held-out gain over the single Gaussian (the 9 `shrink5` fits and 18 control fits). Reported without a level, as declared.
- **Certified share, reported:** 9 of 72 `shrink5` fits certified (12.5%); 9 of the 16 eligible (56%); the certificate adds 0, 4 and 5 publishable fits of 24 at 30, 60 and 120 steps (7 -> 7, 8 -> 12, 9 -> 14).

### E5, the negative controls: the prior controls are NOT valid, so E5 is reported as "no valid prior control; only `personal`" (Part 5b)
- Validity recomputed on the evaluation seeds (shared-only held-out score under the control prior minus under the true prior, needs at least 1 nat/step worse): scrambled **+0.279** (as good as or better than the true prior), shifted **-0.564** (only half a nat worse). **Neither prior control is a wrong prior on these seeds.** The check I declared found this on development for the scrambled prior; for the shifted prior it passed on development (-1.054) and failed on the evaluation seeds (-0.564).
- Certified shares of the controls, all reported: `scrambled` 14/22 eligible (64%), `shifted` 3/9 (33%), `personal` 1/56 (2%), against `shrink5` 9/16 (56%). Judged on the only control that is valid (`personal`, no prior): MET (2% is at most 10% and at most half of 56%). **Over all three controls the criterion is NOT MET** (`scrambled` 64% and `shifted` 33% exceed the levels). The held-out gain of every certified control fit was positive (+1.0 to +2.7 over the single Gaussian): these fits did predict better than a single Gaussian, so the certificate was not wrong to certify them; it just means these controls do not test whether a wrong prior is caught.
- **What this means:** the evaluation shows that the certificate does not certify a no-prior fit (1 of 56) and that every fit it certified predicted better than a single Gaussian (27/27). It does **not** show that a fit anchored to a *harmful* prior would be refused: no harmful prior control was built that was actually harmful on these data. That is **UNKNOWN**, and it is the same lesson as A4 (a control must be able to fail). A valid prior control needs a prior that is measurably worse than the true prior (for example a prior from a different generator); it is WP10 work.

## Reading, stated plainly
- The certificate **works as a conservative add-on where there is something to certify** (9 of the 16 eligible `shrink5` fits at 60 and 120 steps certified, all with a positive held-out gain, none at 30 steps where only 4 fits were eligible and 1 had too few rows) and lifts the publishable share from 29-38% to 29-58% depending on length.
- It does **not** carry a cold-start claim by itself: the declared "survives" sentence and the net deployment gain are not shown at 30 steps, the shortest record, where cold start matters most; and the share of `shrink5` fits that EM publishes (29-38%) is the binding limit, because most fits are withheld for reasons the certificate may not override (degenerate duration, collapse) or are already converged.
- B1's own rule for z did not separate the controls on development, and on the evaluation seeds the controls turned out not to be wrong priors, so the margin 3.0 is the conservative N18 margin, **not** a calibrated one.

## By-product: re-certification by a longer record (RUN, not a declared level)
Shrink5 fits withheld at 30 steps: 17 of 24; of these, publishable at 60 steps: 6; at 120 steps: 9 (same seeds with a longer record; the records are separate fits, not prefixes of one another, so this is an indication of how a growing record helps, not a measured re-certification). B6 (`recertify`) tested the mechanism itself: `tests/WP9_B5_B6_RESULTS.md`.

## What this does not show
Real users and real priors; the generator's centre is the prior's centre (favourable); K = 2, F = 4. A certified fit means "predicted better than a single Gaussian out of sample on these rolling origins", not "the regimes are right". With 9 certified `shrink5` fits and 27 in all, the precision of 27/27 has a lower Wilson bound of 88%.
