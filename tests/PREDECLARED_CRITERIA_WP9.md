## 3. Generator strata (an ASSUMPTION of the study, not a measurement; real separation of regimes is UNKNOWN)
The WP8 generator (`data/synthetic_identity.py`) draws each user's planted separation `sep` log-uniformly in `[sep_lo, sep_hi]` within-regime sd along the activity order. WP9 uses four named strata through `IdentitySpec(sep_lo, sep_hi)` (nothing else in the generator changes, K = 3, 150 days): **hard** [0.4, 0.9], **medium** [0.9, 1.6], **easy** [1.6, 3.0], and the WP8 default **wp8** [0.5, 2.0] (kept for comparison with the WP8 numbers). The strata widths are my choice; they cover less separation than the WP8 default at the bottom and more at the top. Which stratum real users resemble is UNKNOWN and is the reason for reporting every result per stratum.

## 4. A1 diagnosis design (read-only; this part is committed before it runs)
Question (brief A1.1): on seeds 9201..9212, how often do two seeds of the same data give different partitions, as a function of the number of restarts (4, 10, 20), the selection rule (best log-likelihood among CONVERGED non-collapsed restarts, the production rule), and the distance of the partitions (adjusted Rand index)? Is a difference due to local optima, to labelling or ordering, or to regime recovery failure?
- **Data:** users 9201..9204 hard, 9205..9208 medium, 9209..9212 easy; windows [0, 90) and [0, 150); baseline engine, K = 3 fixed, 60 EM iterations, production fit code restart by restart (`integration_report/wp9/scripts/wp9_fit_lib.py`; the selected restart is bit-identical to `fit_hssm` for the same seeds, test `tests/test_wp9_fit_lib.py`). Two fit seeds per window: `user` and `user + 1000`. 20 restarts per fit; the 4- and 10-restart results are the first 4 and the first 10 restarts of the same run (that is exactly what a run with that many restarts does, the seeds are consecutive). 48 fits, about 12 minutes on 2 cores, background, resumable.
- **Measured for each (window, restarts n in 4, 10, 20):** the two selected partitions' ARI; the share of pairs with ARI >= 0.99 ("same partition"), >= 0.9, and below 0.9; the log-likelihood gap between the two selected restarts; whether each selected partition recovers the planted one (ARI to the planted labels >= 0.6 is "recovers"; this is the WP8 convention `recoverable`, kept); the agreement among the best 3 restarts within one fit (minimum pairwise ARI) and the number of distinct partitions (ARI < 0.9) among converged non-collapsed restarts.
- **Classification of a disagreeing pair (ARI < 0.99):** (a) *local optima*: the two selected log-likelihoods differ by more than 1 nat and the better one's partition also recovers the planted regimes or is the higher-likelihood one; (b) *recovery failure*: neither partition recovers the planted regimes (both ARI to truth < 0.6); (c) *labelling / ordering*: the partitions are the same (ARI >= 0.99) but the stable names differ (reported from the WP8 lineage when the same data are replayed; not computed in this diagnosis, listed only as the case that remains when (a) and (b) are excluded and ARI is high). The classification is descriptive; it chooses nothing.
- **What the diagnosis may decide:** the number of restarts and the ARI level that the consensus rule of Part 2 will declare. It may not decide an evaluation criterion. The levels of Part 2 are declared with their reasons before any development or evaluation fit is run, and the evaluation seeds are not touched by the diagnosis.
