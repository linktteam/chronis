## 2. Item 2: K selection with the collapse rule, baseline model (written 2026-11-24, before any scored seed of 7801..7824 ran)

**What I had seen before writing this.** (a) The code of `select_k_by_bic`, `fit_with_random_restarts`, `collapse_report`, `GaussianHSMM.n_params` and the M-step of `A` (READ). (b) ONE development run: dataset seed 7601 (planted K = 3, "easy" design, 15 s): selected K = 3, `CONVERGED`; nothing else about it was looked at. (c) The lost session's partial K-selection run (19 of 72 users) is not in this container and I did not use it. **Not looked at:** any result on seeds 7801..7824.

**2.1 P1-01 (the degrees of freedom), derived from the code, before any run.** `GaussianHSMM` parameters: initial distribution `pi` (re-estimated in the M-step; K - 1 free); transition matrix `A` with the diagonal forced to zero (`np.fill_diagonal(new_A, 0.0)` and row normalisation: the dwell time is carried by the duration law, there are no self-loops), so each row has K - 1 non-zero entries that sum to 1: **K - 2 free per row, K(K - 2) in total (K = 2: A is fixed, 0 free)**; means K*F; diagonal variances K*F (`var` holds per-feature variances); lognormal duration mu and sigma, 2K. Free count = (K-1) + K(K-2) + 2KF + 2K. The code's `n_params()` = (K-1) + **K(K-1)** + 2KF + 2K, i.e. **it over-counts by K**. With F = 7: code 35 / 56 / 79 against free 33 / 53 / 75 for K = 2 / 3 / 4. The BIC penalty is therefore too large by K*ln(n) (ln 75 = 4.3: 8.6 / 13.0 / 17.3 in BIC units), and the penalty difference between K and K - 1 is too large by ln(n) = 4.3.
**What I will do with it (fixed now):** report the table and the effect on planted-K recovery (2.3); add the free count behind a documented switch (class attribute `n_params_convention`, `"legacy"` default = the current count, `"free"` = K(K-2)); the default stays `"legacy"` whatever the result, because a change that moves model selection needs the owner's approval; if the study shows the switch changes the selected K for at least one dataset I state that explicitly and ask for the decision.

**2.2 Design.** Baseline `fit_hssm(candidate_ks=(2, 3, 4), n_initializations=10, max_iter=60, timestamps = days)` (the integration run used `max_iter = 60`). 24 datasets: seeds 7801..7808 planted K = 2, 7809..7816 planted K = 3, 7817..7824 planted K = 4, F = 7, T = 75, segments of 9 to 22 rows, z-scored (the case of `scripts/repro_collapse.py`). Two designs on the same seeds (paired): **easy** (means up to 2.5, noise 0.5: well separated) and **hard** (means up to 1.25, noise 0.8: regimes overlap; chosen so that the choice between K values is not trivial). Script `integration_report/wp7r/scripts/k_select_study.py` (resumable, one line per dataset in `wp7r_evidence/item2_kselect.jsonl`); it records, for every candidate K, every restart's state, log-likelihood and minimum effective days, so the selection can be re-derived offline for other thresholds and formulas (2.4, 2.3) without refitting. Analysis `integration_report/wp7r/scripts/analyze_k_select.py`.

**2.3 Quantities and words (per design; counts with Wilson 95% intervals).**
* **Planted-K recovery:** share of datasets whose served K equals the planted K, by planted K. Word: **"BIC recovers the planted K"** only if at least 20 of the 24 datasets are correct AND each planted K has at least 5 of 8 correct; otherwise "BIC does not reliably recover the planted K" (with the pattern). (Reason: 20 of 24 = 83% is the level at which a one-sided binomial test against a coin-flip-among-three guess, p0 = 1/3, is far below 0.001; 5 of 8 per planted K prevents a pooled pass that hides one failing K.)
* **P1-01 effect:** the K that BIC would select with the free count (offline, from the stored per-K log-likelihoods and the same demotion rule): number of datasets whose selected K changes, and recovery under the free count. Word: **"the count moves model selection"** if at least one dataset changes (then: owner approval, default stays); else "the count does not move selection in this study".
* **Collapse handling:** (i) hard criterion: **0 served collapsed fits** (a served fit has every regime >= 4 effective days; a collapsed selected model must be withheld with `collapsed_regime`); (ii) hard criterion: a collapsed candidate K is never selected while a non-collapsed, healthy candidate exists (checked from the stored per-K collapse flags); (iii) descriptive: in how many datasets some candidate K is collapsed, in how many the lowest-BIC K is collapsed (the demotion changes the choice), and in those datasets whether the demoted choice or the raw-BIC choice equals the planted K. Word: **"demotion helps"** if in at least one dataset the raw BIC choice is wrong and the demoted choice is the planted K, and never the reverse; **"demotion harms"** if the reverse happens at least once and never the helpful case; "mixed" if both; "never triggered" if the demotion changes no dataset.
* **Served ARI at the selected K** (argmax of the filtered posterior against the planted path): median, min and the list for the served (not withheld) fits; descriptive for the withheld. Word: **"served ARI is adequate"** if the median over served fits is at least 0.80 in the easy design (hard: reported only).
* **Withheld rate and reasons** per design and planted K.

**2.4 Sensitivity to the 4-day rule (measurement only; the rule is not changed).** The offline re-derivation for thresholds 3, 4 and 6 effective days: per K the best converged non-collapsed restart (else the best converged one), its BIC, collapse flag and the demotion rule, then the selected K; and the served/withheld status (withheld by `collapsed_regime` when the selected model's minimum effective days is below the threshold). Validity gate: the re-derivation at 4 days must reproduce the actual selected K and the actual collapsed flag of all 48 datasets; if it does not, the sensitivity is NOT claimed (it is reported as unverified). Reported per threshold: planted-K recovery, withheld-by-collapse rate, number of datasets whose selected K differs from the 4-day choice. The ARI at 3 and 6 days is not reported (the labels of an alternative restart are not stored).

**2.5 What this item does not do.** No change to the threshold, to the demotion rule, to restart selection or to any numeric output of `fit_hssm`; the only code change is the opt-in `n_params_convention` switch whose default reproduces the current numbers bit for bit (to be verified by a test).

## 4. Item 4: N17, a practical route for `m_t_std` (written 2026-11-24, before any scored seed of 9101..9112 ran; time-boxed; NO engine change, NO integration)

**What I had seen before writing this.** (a) `tests/WP5_N17_DIAGNOSIS.md` and `tests/WP6_N17_RESULT.md` (READ): WP5 shrinkage coverage 0.928 / 0.933 per latent dimension (24 seeds, 20 iterations, a prior centred on the generator: a favourable case), personal 0.675 / 0.677, true parameters 0.951 / 0.950; WP6 delta-method term E0 0.716 -> E1 0.818 at +-2 (seeds 7401..7424, not a pass), 90 s for the Hessian plus 9 s for the Jacobian at P = 50 continuous parameters, cost growing with P^2. (b) The code of `n17_proto.py` (pack / unpack / numeric Hessian), `n17_diag.py` (the truth model, the coverage statistic), `data/synthetic_dual.py` (the generator: it has NO sampler for arbitrary parameters; the bootstrap needs one, written in the script). (c) From Item 3 (stage B was running while this was written): only counts of withheld fits per seed in the log, no score. (d) No seed 9101..9112 has been run.

**4.1 Common design.** Seeds 9101..9112 (12), the N17 generator `generate_synthetic_dual_timescale_data(n_regimes=2, n_features=4, latent_dim=2, n_segments=4, mean_dwell=15.0, seed)`, `K = 2, F = 4, L = 2, max_duration = 25`. Statistic exactly as WP5/WP6: the per-true-regime in-sample affine map of the true latent state into the fitted coordinates (`per_regime_affine_prediction(true_m_t, m_hat, true_p_t, min_points = 3 (L + 1))`), `z = (m_hat - mapped truth) / m_t_std`; `c2` = share with `|z| <= 2`, `c1` = share with `|z| <= 1`, averaged over the two latent dimensions; seed-level values are averaged over seeds. Nominal 0.9545 (+-2) and 0.6827 (+-1). **Band** = nominal +- 0.04 = [0.9145, 0.9945] and [0.6427, 0.7227], the WP6 B1 band, unchanged (the brief names +-0.04; no scale factor is tuned anywhere in this item). Harness control `T0`: the engine at the generator's true parameters (WP5 `truth_model`, no alignment): if its mean `c2` is outside the band the harness is invalid and nothing below is read.

**4.2 Part (a): coverage for shrinkage-fitted fits that are publishable.** Arms, all through the public path as in Item 3 (`fit_hssm(model_type="dual_timescale", n_initializations=1, allow_fast_test_fit=True, random_seed = seed - 2000, ...)`, `m_t`, `m_t_std` from `estimate_states` on the fitted model, publishable = `not insights_withheld`, read before `estimate_states`): `shrink5` at `max_iter = 200` (the budget at which Item 3 stage B can produce a publishable fit) and at 20 (the WP5 setting, always withheld in Item 3 stage A); `personal` at 200 and at 20 (the WP6 `E0` setting); `T0`. Reported per arm: withheld count; mean `c2`, `c1` over ALL fits (the WP5-comparable number) and over the PUBLISHABLE fits; seeds below 0.80. **Word:** "coverage of publishable shrinkage-fitted fits reaches the band" only if at least **8** publishable shrinkage fits (`max_iter = 200`) exist AND both means (`c2`, `c1`) are inside the band; "outside the band" if judged and not; "not judged" otherwise (8 for the same reason as Item 3: below it the standard error of a mean over seeds, about 0.03 to 0.08 here, is as large as the band half-width). The WP5 0.93 is a favourable-case number (prior centred on the generator) and is reported as such, whatever the result.

**4.3 Part (b): parametric bootstrap on a small subset.** Seeds 9101..9106 (the first six; chosen by seed number before any result), fit `personal` at 20 iterations (the WP6 N17 setting, so the numbers are comparable with `E0`/`E1` of WP6; these fits are `MAX_ITER_REACHED` and NOT publishable: the question here is only whether the route can close the calibration gap). For each seed: `B = 30` replicates; replicate `b` = simulate a series of the SAME length from the fitted parameters (a sampler written in the script: regime path from `pi`, `A_slow` with zero diagonal and the fitted lognormal durations truncated at `max_duration`; latent state continuous across segments from `N(m0_k, P0_k)` with regime-dependent AR(1) `A_fast, b_fast, Q_fast`; emissions `C_obs m + d_obs + N(0, R_obs)`), refit with the SAME code and iteration count started from the fitted parameters (warm start, so that the latent frame is nearly the same), compute the smoothed mean of the ORIGINAL series under the replicate's parameters, map it into the original fit's coordinates by the per-regime in-sample affine map using the original fit's MAP regime labels (output-space frame removal, as in WP6 amendment 7.5), and take the variance over replicates. `m_t_std_boot^2 = m_t_std^2 + that variance`. Reasons: `B = 30` gives a relative standard error of about 13% on each standard deviation (`1 / sqrt(2 (B - 1))`), which moves a +-2 coverage by one or two points; warm start because a cold refit lands in an arbitrary latent frame and the frame removal would then dominate. Reported: `E0` (unchanged `m_t_std`) and `EB` coverage `c2`, `c1`, per seed and mean; paired difference `EB - E0` (mean, paired se, seeds improved / 6); the mean ratio `m_t_std_boot / m_t_std`; wall time per replicate fit and per seed. **Word:** "the bootstrap reaches the band" only if the mean `c2` and `c1` of `EB` over the 6 seeds are both inside the band; "improves but does not reach" if `EB - E0 > 0` on at least 5 of 6 seeds and not both inside the band; "does not help" otherwise. Six seeds is a screening size: a result "reaches" is a reason to evaluate on fresh seeds, never a pass of the N17 criterion.

**4.4 Part (c): cost.** Measured, not argued: for `P = 50` (`K = 2, F = 4, L = 2`) and for `P = 204` (`K = 3, F = 10, L = 3`; the count `K L^2 + K L + K L (L + 1)/2 + K F L + K F + K F` is 50 and 204), on one generator series with `n_segments = 4`: seconds per forward pass (`loglik`), per `estimate_states`, per 20-iteration fit, the number of forward passes the numeric Hessian of the WP6 prototype needs (`P^2/2 + 2P + 1`), and the full Hessian + Jacobian time MEASURED at `P = 50` (one seed); at `P = 204` the Hessian time is EXTRAPOLATED as passes times the measured per-pass time and labelled so (a full run would take hours). Also the bootstrap cost per fit: `B` times the per-fit seconds (measured at both sizes).

**4.5 Recommendation rule (fixed now).** In this order: (1) if the bootstrap "reaches the band" (4.3) and its cost per fit at `P = 50` is at most 15 minutes: **"an offline bootstrap job"**, to be evaluated on fresh seeds before any integration; (2) else if 4.2 is "reaches the band" (judged, n >= 8): **"shrinkage-only uncertainty"**, with the favourable-case caveat; (3) else if the bootstrap "improves but does not reach": **"continue"** (what to try next is named from the numbers); (4) else **"keep numeric confidence hidden"** (the status quo of the contract document 9.4). The xfail on the N17 cells stays in every case: nothing is integrated in this item, and a route can only pass the N17 floor in a later package.

**5.1 What is built (dual model only; the baseline model and every default are unchanged).**
* **R1 `collapsed_regime` for the dual model.** Effective days of regime `k` = the sum over rows of the FILTERED regime posterior `p_t_posterior[:, k]` at the final parameters (the posterior the result already carries; the dual model returns no smoothed regime posterior). * **R2 `degenerate_duration`.** A regime is degenerate iff (i) its duration parameters are at a wall of the box (the existing `duration_at_bound` flag: mu or sigma within `DUAL_DURATION_BOUND_TOL` of a wall, which includes sigma at its wall 3.0) or (ii) its duration pmf (the truncated, renormalised lognormal on 1..Dmax, `duration_pmf`) is STILL RISING at the last lattice step: `pmf[Dmax] > pmf[Dmax - 1]`. New fields `fit_duration_degenerate` (bool) and `duration_degenerate_report` (per regime flags and the numbers); new withheld reason `degenerate_duration: ...`. `DURATION_BOX_REFIT` is unchanged and still runs first.

Dual model without a shared prior only (a `ValueError` otherwise, and for a value that is not an integer larger than `max_duration`).
