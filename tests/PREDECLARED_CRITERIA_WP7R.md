## 1. Item 1: does more EM make the dual model predict worse? (written 2026-11-17, before any scored seed ran)

**What I had seen before writing this.** (a) `02_WP7_SALVAGE_NOTES.md` (REPORTED by the lost session): all 8 N18 fits at 20 iterations were `MAX_ITER_REACHED`; a continuation of 10 stage-2 steps raised the training log-likelihood by 0.9 to 3.4 nats and changed the mean held-out step score by -0.547, -0.898, -0.096, +0.010, +0.088, -0.011, -0.092, -0.115 (mean -0.21), i.e. worse in 6 of 8; WP6 saw coverage fall with more iterations (0.826 to 0.692, `tests/WP6_N17_RESULT.md`). (b) The code of `DualTimescaleHSSM.fit` / `_fit_impl` (READ). (c) ONE wall-time measurement on development seed 7601 (20 iterations, T = 256 rows, 11.6 s uncontended; no score of any kind was computed or looked at). **Not looked at:** any held-out score, training log-likelihood or duration parameter on the N18 seeds or on seeds 5201..5220 under the checkpoint design below.

**1.1 Design.** Two seed groups, nothing excluded: **N18** = the 8 official N18 seeds (202, 404, 505, 606, 707, 808, 909, 1010; `tests/utils/heldout_prediction.continued_data`: K = 2, F = 4, L = 2, 4 training + 2 held-out segments, T about 60, model seed = data seed) and **D-pop** = seeds 5201..5220 (`generate_population_user(seed, 12, 2)`: 12 training + 2 held-out segments, T between about 150 and 260, model seed = seed + 2000, as in WP6). Model `DualTimescaleHSSM(n_regimes=2, n_features=4, latent_dim=2, max_duration=25)`.
One EM trajectory per seed to 160 iterations with `DURATION_BOX_REFIT` off (the pure EM path: "more iterations" is literally the same path continued), parameters snapshotted after the 20th, 40th, 80th and 160th M-step. This equals `fit(n_iter = N)` for N = 20, 40, 80, 160 because the loop does not depend on N (a fit that stops early by `abs_tol` or by the guard returns the same parameters for every larger N; its final state stands in for the missing checkpoints and is flagged `reached = false`). Script: `integration_report/wp7r/scripts/item1_iter_study.py`; one JSON line per seed (resumable) in `wp7r_evidence/item1_iter.jsonl`.
**Statistic (as N18):** forward filter over train + held-out with the checkpoint's parameters frozen; mean one-step-ahead predictive log density per held-out step. Alongside: training log-likelihood per row at the checkpoint, the single-Gaussian score fitted on the training rows (the N18 baseline), and whether the durations are outside the WP6 box.
**(F) Faithfulness check (cheap, 8 N18 seeds):** the shipped path (`fit(n_iter = 20)` with the box refit on; `tests/utils/heldout_prediction.engine_scores("real", seed)`, 25 iterations for seed 202 as N18 does) against the 20-iteration checkpoint of the same seed. Report how many of the 8 held-out scores are bit-identical; a difference is expected only where the 20-iteration fit ended outside the box (the refit), and those seeds are listed.

**1.2 Reported quantities.** For each group and pooled: mean held-out score at each checkpoint; the paired difference (later minus earlier) for 20 to 40, 40 to 80, 80 to 160 and 20 to 160: mean, paired standard error (sd/sqrt(n)), n, number of seeds favouring more iterations (difference > 0); the same for the training log-likelihood per row; the share of seeds whose dual held-out score beats the single Gaussian at each checkpoint (descriptive; it informs Item 5c).

**1.3 Words (fixed now).** Per seed, the OLS slope of the held-out score on log2(iterations) over the 4 checkpoints (units: nats per step per doubling). Let S = the mean slope over the 28 seeds, se its standard error.
* **"More EM lowers the held-out score (a trend)"** only if ALL hold: S < 0 and S/se <= -2; at least 20 of the 28 slopes are negative; the mean slope is negative in each of the two groups.
* **"More EM raises the held-out score"** only if the mirror image holds (S > 0 and S/se >= 2; at least 20 positive; both group means positive).
* Otherwise **"no consistent trend"**, reported with the pattern by step and group.
Reasons: 2 se rather than the 3 se of N18/WP5 because the consequence (Item 5c, an opt-in gate that is off by default) is reversible, so a false positive is cheap; 20 of 28 is a one-sided binomial p of 0.018 (RUN), so a sign share that high is unlikely by chance; both groups because the two generators and the training lengths differ (T about 60 against 150 to 260), and a decline in one group only would be a property of that design. "6 of 8" alone (p = 0.14, RUN) is explicitly not enough.

**1.4 Salvage-note facts (each marked RE-VERIFIED or NOT RE-VERIFIED in the report; thresholds fixed now).**
* **S1, EM creeps (rate about 0.995 per iteration; about 1,330 iterations to |dll| < 1e-4).** From the E-step log-likelihood history of the 160-iteration runs: per seed the median over iterations 100 to 160 of r = (ll[t+1]-ll[t]) / (ll[t]-ll[t-1]) (only while both increments are positive), then the median over seeds; the extrapolated iteration count at which the increment would fall below 1e-4 is 160 + ln(1e-4 / d160) / ln(r), d160 = the last increment. RE-VERIFIED if the median r lies in [0.99, 0.999] and the median extrapolated count in [700, 2500] (a factor of about two around 1,330, since the seeds differ). Seeds that stop by `abs_tol` before 160 are counted as "converged" and left out of the extrapolation. **The "99% of the remaining gain is in the observation block" part is NOT attempted** (it needs the Q-block decomposition of the M-step, which is not cheap): NOT RE-VERIFIED.
* **S2, segments longer than Dmax go with degenerate durations (Fisher p = 0.015 in the lost session).** On the 28 seeds: "degenerate" = durations outside the WP6 box at checkpoint 160; "long" = at least one planted training segment longer than Dmax = 25. Fisher exact test (two-sided) on the 2 x 2 table. RE-VERIFIED if p < 0.05 and the odds ratio is above 1; the lost session's p is not claimed to be reproduced to the digit.
* **S3, Dmax = 60 cured 8 of 9.** For the seeds degenerate at 160 iterations with Dmax = 25 (at most the first 12 by seed): the same trajectory with `max_duration = 60`, 160 iterations; "cured" = durations inside the box for Dmax = 60 (mu <= ln(10 * 60)). RE-VERIFIED if at least 75% of those seeds are cured and there are at least 5 of them; with fewer than 5, NOT RE-VERIFIED (too few). Cost: a Dmax = 60 lattice is about 2.4 times slower per iteration; if the number of seeds makes the estimate exceed 15 minutes I stop and report the estimate first.

**1.5 What this item decides.** Item 5c (the dual publish gate) is built only if 1.3 says "more EM lowers the held-out score". Nothing in the engine changes in Item 1.

## 2. Item 2: K selection with the collapse rule, baseline model (written 2026-11-24, before any scored seed of 7801..7824 ran)

**What I had seen before writing this.** (a) The code of `select_k_by_bic`, `fit_with_random_restarts`, `collapse_report`, `GaussianHSMM.n_params` and the M-step of `A` (READ). (b) ONE development run: dataset seed 7601 (planted K = 3, "easy" design, 15 s): selected K = 3, `CONVERGED`; nothing else about it was looked at. (c) The lost session's partial K-selection run (19 of 72 users) is not in this container and I did not use it. **Not looked at:** any result on seeds 7801..7824.

**2.1 P1-01 (the degrees of freedom), derived from the code, before any run.** `GaussianHSMM` parameters: initial distribution `pi` (re-estimated in the M-step; K - 1 free); transition matrix `A` with the diagonal forced to zero (`np.fill_diagonal(new_A, 0.0)` and row normalisation: the dwell time is carried by the duration law, there are no self-loops), so each row has K - 1 non-zero entries that sum to 1: **K - 2 free per row, K(K - 2) in total (K = 2: A is fixed, 0 free)**; means K*F; diagonal variances K*F (`var` holds per-feature variances); lognormal duration mu and sigma, 2K. Free count = (K-1) + K(K-2) + 2KF + 2K. The code's `n_params()` = (K-1) + **K(K-1)** + 2KF + 2K, i.e. **it over-counts by K**. With F = 7: code 35 / 56 / 79 against free 33 / 53 / 75 for K = 2 / 3 / 4. The BIC penalty is therefore too large by K*ln(n) (ln 75 = 4.3: 8.6 / 13.0 / 17.3 in BIC units), and the penalty difference between K and K - 1 is too large by ln(n) = 4.3.
**What I will do with it (fixed now):** report the table and the effect on planted-K recovery (2.3); add the free count behind a documented switch (class attribute `n_params_convention`, `"legacy"` default = the current count, `"free"` = K(K-2)); the default stays `"legacy"` whatever the result, because a change that moves model selection needs the owner's approval; if the study shows the switch changes the selected K for at least one dataset I state that explicitly and ask for the decision.

**2.2 Design.** Baseline `fit_hssm(candidate_ks=(2, 3, 4), n_initializations=10, max_iter=60, timestamps = days)` (the integration run used `max_iter = 60`). 24 datasets: seeds 7801..7808 planted K = 2, 7809..7816 planted K = 3, 7817..7824 planted K = 4, F = 7, T = 75, segments of 9 to 22 rows, z-scored (the case of `scripts/repro_collapse.py`). Two designs on the same seeds (paired): **easy** (means up to 2.5, noise 0.5: well separated) and **hard** (means up to 1.25, noise 0.8: regimes overlap; chosen so that the choice between K values is not trivial). Script `integration_report/wp7r/scripts/k_select_study.py` (resumable, one line per dataset in `wp7r_evidence/item2_kselect.jsonl`); it records, for every candidate K, every restart's state, log-likelihood and minimum effective days, so the selection can be re-derived offline for other thresholds and formulas (2.4, 2.3) without refitting. Analysis `integration_report/wp7r/scripts/analyze_k_select.py`.

**2.3 Quantities and words (per design; counts with Wilson 95% intervals).**
* **Planted-K recovery:** share of datasets whose served K equals the planted K, by planted K. Word: **"BIC recovers the planted K"** only if at least 20 of the 24 datasets are correct AND each planted K has at least 5 of 8 correct; otherwise "BIC does not reliably recover the planted K" (with the pattern). (Reason: 20 of 24 = 83% is the level at which a one-sided binomial test against a coin-flip-among-three guess, p0 = 1/3, is far below 0.001; 5 of 8 per planted K prevents a pooled pass that hides one failing K.)
* **P1-01 effect:** the K that BIC would select with the free count (offline, from the stored per-K log-likelihoods and the same demotion rule): number of datasets whose selected K changes, and recovery under the free count. Word: **"the count moves model selection"** if at least one dataset changes (then: owner approval, default stays); else "the count does not move selection in this study".
* **Collapse handling:** (i) hard criterion: **0 served collapsed fits** (a served fit has every regime >= 4 effective days; a collapsed selected model must be withheld with `collapsed_regime`); (ii) hard criterion: a collapsed candidate K is never selected while a non-collapsed, healthy candidate exists (checked from the stored per-K collapse flags); (iii) descriptive: in how many datasets some candidate K is collapsed, in how many the lowest-BIC K is collapsed (the demotion changes the choice), and in those datasets whether the demoted choice or the raw-BIC choice equals the planted K. Word: **"demotion helps"** if in at least one dataset the raw BIC choice is wrong and the demoted choice is the planted K, and never the reverse; **"demotion harms"** if the reverse happens at least once and never the helpful case; "mixed" if both; "never triggered" if the demotion changes no dataset.
* **Served ARI at the selected K** (argmax of the filtered posterior against the planted path): median, min and the list for the served (not withheld) fits; descriptive for the withheld. Word: **"served ARI is adequate"** if the median over served fits is at least 0.80 in the easy design (hard: reported only).
* **Withheld rate and reasons** per design and planted K.

**2.4 Sensitivity to the 4-day rule (measurement only; the rule is not changed).** The offline re-derivation for thresholds 3, 4 and 6 effective days: per K the best converged non-collapsed restart (else the best converged one), its BIC, collapse flag and the demotion rule, then the selected K; and the served/withheld status (withheld by `collapsed_regime` when the selected model's minimum effective days is below the threshold). Validity gate: the re-derivation at 4 days must reproduce the actual selected K and the actual collapsed flag of all 48 datasets; if it does not, the sensitivity is NOT claimed (it is reported as unverified). Reported per threshold: planted-K recovery, withheld-by-collapse rate, number of datasets whose selected K differs from the 4-day choice. The ARI at 3 and 6 days is not reported (the labels of an alternative restart are not stored).

**2.5 What this item does not do.** No change to the threshold, to the demotion rule, to restart selection or to any numeric output of `fit_hssm`; the only code change is the opt-in `n_params_convention` switch whose default reproduces the current numbers bit for bit (to be verified by a test).

## 3. Item 3: shrinkage (cold start) under fail-closed (written 2026-11-24, before any scored seed of 7901..7924 ran)

**What I had seen before writing this.** (a) `n18v2_study.py` and `tests/utils/n18v2.py` of WP5 (READ): the arms, the lengths 30/60/120 with 2/4/8 training segments and 2 held-out segments, `N_ITER = 20`, the scoring and the paired statistic. (b) The STORED WP5 evaluation fits (`eval_results.jsonl`, seeds 7001..7024, a different set from this item's seeds): fit states and wall times only: `personal` 72 of 72 `MAX_ITER_REACHED`; `shrink@main@5` 46 `MAX_ITER_REACHED`, 25 `REJECTED_UPDATE`, 1 `CONVERGED`. I had NOT computed withheld-versus-score tables from them. (c) ONE development probe on seed 7602 (development only), length 60: the public path `fit_hssm(model_type="dual_timescale", ...)` and the WP5 class path give a bit-identical held-out score for `personal` and for `shrink5` at 20 and at 200 iterations; `personal` is `MAX_ITER_REACHED` at both caps (3 s and 30 s); `shrink5` stops after 3 iterations with `REJECTED_UPDATE` at both caps (so a larger cap alone does not change it on that user). I looked at fit states, timings and equality, not at an arm comparison. (d) No score of any seed 7901..7924, nor of 7601..7612 other than that probe, has been looked at. Seeds 7601..7612 stay development only.

**What this item is for.** WP5 found that shrinkage toward a population prior beats a single Gaussian at 30/60/120 training steps (24 seeds, `mean - 3 se > 0` at all three lengths). WP5 did not apply the WP6 rule "a fit that is not `CONVERGED` is withheld". Item 3 asks whether the gain exists on the fits a user would actually be served.

**3.1 Design (nothing excluded).** Seeds 7901..7924, each at lengths 30, 60, 120 (`generate_population_user(seed, n_segments in {2, 4, 8}, 2, PopulationSpec())`, `K = 2, F = 4, L = 2`, `max_duration = 25`, as WP5). Arms:
* `gauss`: the single Gaussian fitted on the training rows (the WP5 reference; no regime model, so no fit state and no insight; never withheld).
* `shared`: the WP5 shared model, no per-user EM (`become_shared_model()`); not a fit, so no fit-state rule applies and it is never withheld (it is reported as such and nobody should read it as "publishable").
* `personal`: one dual-timescale fit, no prior (WP5's `personal`: ONE restart; the production default is 10 restarts with a preference for a converged restart, which is NOT tested here).
* `shrink5`: the dual fit with the shared prior at the frozen n0 = 5.0 (`ShrinkageDualHSSM`, prior = the WP5 frozen `prior_main.json`, built from users 8001..8020, copied to `wp7r_evidence/prior_main_wp5.json`, sha256 `6a15da4b539eba89c57f58616668d7e998e5cf564680963aedd64d3d05f8952e`; the prior is NOT rebuilt and n0 is NOT re-tuned).
`personal` and `shrink5` are fitted through the PUBLIC path `fit_hssm(model_type="dual_timescale", candidate_ks=(2,), n_initializations=1, allow_fast_test_fit=True, random_seed=seed - 2000, max_duration=25, latent_dim=2[, shared_prior=PRIOR, shared_prior_n0=5.0], config=ColdStartConfig(min_present_sessions=1))`, so "withheld" is the real `HSSMResult.insights_withheld` / `withheld_reason`, read immediately after the fit and BEFORE scoring (scoring can add fallbacks to the model counter). `random_seed = seed - 2000` makes the model seed equal the user seed as in WP5. The cold-start gate is switched off for this study ONLY (`min_present_sessions=1`; the default and the threshold are unchanged): at length 30 the training sets have 17..60 rows and the production gate (30 present sessions) would refuse the shorter ones whatever the prior; that is reported (3.3, eligibility), not hidden.
**Stages.** **A** = the WP5 design: `max_iter = 20` (WP5's `N_ITER`), all four arms, all 24 seeds. **B** = the production budget `max_iter = 200` (the `fit_hssm` default), arms `personal` and `shrink5` only, all 24 seeds (`gauss` and `shared` do not depend on the cap). Stage A is run and reported first; B is run afterwards in the background, resumable, results appended per seed. Reason for two stages: at 20 iterations the stored WP5 fits are almost never `CONVERGED`, so A alone could only say "withheld"; B asks whether the answer changes at the budget a product would use. Cost estimate (from the stored WP5 timings and the probe): A about 5 minutes on 2 cores; B up to about 40 minutes (the `personal` fit at 200 iterations took 30 s at 72 rows).
**Score.** As N18/WP5: forward filter over train + held-out with parameters frozen; mean one-step-ahead predictive log density per held-out step (`tests/utils/n18v2.score_model`). A non-finite score is dropped from a paired statistic and COUNTED; nothing is imputed.

**3.2 Reported quantities (per stage, per length, per arm).**
1. The withheld tally: fits, withheld, withheld rate, by `fit_state` and by reason prefix (`tests/utils/item3_stats.withheld_tally`), plus iterations run, `convergence_criterion`, `window_gain_at_cap`.
2. **Publishable-fit statistic:** on the seeds where the arm is NOT withheld: mean held-out score of the arm and of `gauss`, and the paired difference arm minus `gauss` (mean, paired se with ddof 1, mean - 3 se, seeds favouring the arm / n). Also arm minus `shared` and (for `shrink5`) minus `personal` on the seeds where BOTH are publishable (n shown).
3. **Risk-coverage (withheld = "no prediction"):** coverage = share of the 24 seeds served; and the difference arm minus `gauss` over ALL 24 seeds where a withheld fit (or a publishable fit with a non-finite score) is replaced by the single Gaussian, so it contributes exactly 0 (mean, paired se, mean - 3 se).
4. For reference only: the WP5 statistic, arm minus `gauss` over all 24 seeds INCLUDING the withheld fits (what WP5 measured; not a statement about publishable fits).
5. Eligibility (descriptive): number of seeds per length with at least 30 training rows (the production gate), and how many of those are also publishable.

**3.3 Words (fixed now).**
* **"The WP5 cold-start gain survives on publishable fits"** (shrink5 versus `gauss`, per stage) uses `item3_stats.survives` on quantity 2 with the SAME floor as WP5 criterion 1: `mean - 3 se > 0` (strictly), at every length. A length is **judged** only if at least **8** publishable fits with finite scores remain. Words: **survives** (all three lengths judged and all pass); **does not survive** (any judged length fails); **partly judged** (the judged lengths pass, at least one length is not judged); **cannot be judged** (no length has 8). Reasons: the floor is the one WP5 used, so the comparison with WP5 is like for like; 8 because at n = 8 the multiplier 3 equals the one-sided 99% t quantile (2.998 with 7 df), and for n < 8 the quantile is larger than 3 (3.14 at n = 7), so "mean - 3 se" would understate the uncertainty; an unjudged length can never veto and never confirm.
* **"Net deployment gain"** (serving the arm where publishable and the single Gaussian elsewhere): **positive** if quantity 3 has `mean - 3 se > 0` at every length; otherwise **not shown**. The mean is always reported.
* **Coverage flag (descriptive, no consequence):** an arm whose coverage is below 50% at a length "withholds most fits" there.
* Nothing is reported as "the cold-start feature works" unless BOTH the first word is "survives" and the second is "positive".
* If a result contradicts a statement of the WP5 report, the report says so in those words; no criterion is changed afterwards (a dated section is added instead).

**3.4 Expectation, written down so it can be wrong (not a criterion).** From the stored WP5 fit states (b) I expect `personal` and `shrink5` to be withheld in nearly every fit at 20 iterations, so that quantity 2 has fewer than 8 publishable fits and the word is "cannot be judged" for stage A. For stage B I have no expectation: `shrink5` may keep stopping early with `REJECTED_UPDATE`, which would make the answer the same.

**3.5 Not done in this item (stated so that nobody reads it as covered).** The atypical-user check of WP5 (criterion 3) is not re-run (the brief does not list it). The prior is the WP5 prior and is not rebuilt at the current head. `personal` uses one restart. The cold-start gate is not changed. No change to the engine is made in this item; the only new code is `tests/utils/item3_stats.py` (pure functions; `tests/test_wp7r_item3_risk_coverage.py`, 8 tests; 22 mutants, all killed: `wp7r_evidence/mutation_item3_stats.txt`) and the study script.
